WHAT WE CAN HELP WITH

Fixture Installation & Repair for your home.

Discuss repairs and installation for kitchen and bathroom faucets, sink connections, shower fixtures, and related accessible plumbing. The exact work depends on the fixture, replacement parts, existing connections, and access beneath or behind the installation.

A drip at the spout is different from water appearing in the cabinet below. Identifying where water first appears helps separate a fixture problem from a supply or drain connection issue. For a new fixture, appearance is only part of the decision: mounting holes, reach, clearances, and compatible connections also matter. Share product details before ordering so the installation can be discussed.

Signs it’s time to get in touch

  • A faucet drips even when it is turned off
  • Moisture collects beneath a sink or around a fixture
  • A handle is stiff, loose, or difficult to operate
  • A replacement fixture needs its connections assessed

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Questions that define the scope.

Do I need a new faucet for a small drip?

Not always. A repair may be possible when suitable parts are available and the fixture is in serviceable condition.

Can you fit a fixture I have purchased?

Confirm compatibility and the installation arrangement with the business first. Some fixtures need different connections or access.

Does fixture installation include countertop or tile work?

Do not assume that finishing work is included. Ask for the installation and any required access or making-good work to be described in the scope.

Is a leak below the sink a faucet problem?

It could involve a supply connection, drain, trap, or fixture. A photo and a description of when the water appears help with the initial conversation.
