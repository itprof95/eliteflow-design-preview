YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Questions that define the scope.

Does one leak mean all my pipes need replacing?

No. The condition and cause need to be assessed. A localized repair and a wider replacement have different scopes and should be discussed using the findings.

Can you repair concealed pipework?

The business must confirm access and the repair scope. Do not assume that a concealed repair can be completed without opening a surface.

Will you repair the wall afterward?

Ask whether access openings and restoration are included or require a separate arrangement.

Can I request a water-main assessment?

Describe the location and symptoms when you call. Confirm whether the concern involves customer pipework, a shared line, or equipment that needs the water supplier’s attention.
