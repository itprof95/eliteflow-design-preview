YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

A few things you might be wondering.

Why does my drain keep clogging?

Build-up, objects in the line, and pipe condition can all play a part. A recurring clog calls for an assessment of the cause, rather than assuming the last blockage has simply returned.

Can you help if more than one drain is backing up?

Yes, request an assessment and mention every affected fixture. Multiple backups may require a sewer or main-line evaluation. For an active overflow, call the emergency line.

Should I mention drain cleaner I have used?

Yes. Tell the plumber the product and when you used it so they can plan the work appropriately.

How much will drain cleaning cost?

The blockage, pipe condition, access, and work needed affect the scope. Call to explain the problem and request an estimate after assessment. No fixed price is listed on this website.
