WHAT WE CAN HELP WITH

Toilet Repair & Installation for your property.

Service can involve tank components, flushing problems, supply connections, leaks around the fixture, and installation of a replacement toilet. A wet floor, a tank that repeatedly refills, and a blockage need different checks; describing the symptoms helps the plumber focus the assessment.

A running toilet may involve a worn tank component, while movement at the base or moisture on the floor calls for a closer look at the fixture and its connection. If you are replacing a toilet, the existing layout, rough-in measurements, shutoff connection, and condition of the flange all affect the installation plan. Ask what is included in removal and disposal of the old fixture.

Signs it’s time to get in touch

  • The tank refills repeatedly when no one has flushed
  • Water appears near the base or supply connection
  • The toilet rocks, flushes weakly, or clogs repeatedly
  • A damaged or outdated fixture is ready for replacement

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Practical answers before the visit.

Can a running toilet be repaired?

Often a tank component can be repaired or replaced, but the correct part depends on the toilet and the source of the problem. An assessment comes first.

Can I supply my own replacement toilet?

Ask before purchasing. The business can discuss the dimensions, connections, installation scope, and arrangements for a customer-supplied fixture.

What if the toilet keeps blocking?

Explain how often it happens and whether other drains are affected. The problem may need drain or sewer-line assessment as well as a fixture check.

Will you remove the old toilet?

Confirm removal, disposal, and any floor or flange work in the proposed scope before the installation is booked.
