YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Practical answers before the visit.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the area immediately. From a safe location, call 911 and your gas utility’s emergency line. Do not wait for a website form response. Follow the utility’s emergency instructions.

Can I request an appliance relocation?

Yes, describe the planned move. The business must assess the proposed connection and confirm what work can be accepted.

Do you work on the utility meter?

Do not assume utility-owned equipment is included. Contact the gas supplier about its meter and confirm the boundary of any proposed plumbing work.

Are permits, inspections, and testing included?

Ask for the applicable requirements and responsibility for each step to be explained in the project scope before work proceeds.
