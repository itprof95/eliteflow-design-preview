WHAT WE CAN HELP WITH

One blocked drain, or a problem farther along the line?

Hair, soap residue, food waste and other buildup can restrict a household drain. A blockage at one sink can require a different approach from several fixtures backing up together. The pattern matters: tell us what happens when you use nearby sinks, toilets or showers.

Drain cleaning focuses on removing the obstruction and checking drainage. If the same problem returns, clearing it again may not address the underlying cause. Pipe condition, access and the location of the restriction help determine whether further investigation or a separate repair is needed. Ask which clearing method is proposed and why it suits your plumbing.

Signs your drain needs attention

  • Water drains slowly or remains in a sink, shower or tub.
  • Gurgling accompanies draining or flushing.
  • The same clog returns after it has been cleared.
  • Water backs up into another fixture, or several drains are affected.

YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Drain cleaning questions

Why does the same drain keep clogging?

A restriction may remain, buildup may return, or the pipe may have another problem. A recurring clog needs an assessment of the cause; it does not automatically mean the entire line needs replacement.

What if more than one drain is backing up?

Several affected fixtures can indicate a shared drain or main-line issue. Describe the full pattern when you call. A separate sewer or main-line assessment may be appropriate.

Do you use a camera or hydro jetting?

The method depends on the plumbing and obstruction. Call to confirm the equipment and options available for your property. No particular tool or technique is promised before that discussion.

How much will drain cleaning cost?

Cost depends on access, the location and type of blockage, and any additional investigation or repair. Ask about assessment charges and the proposed scope before authorizing work.
