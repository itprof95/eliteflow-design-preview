YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Practical answers before the visit.

Do multiple slow drains always mean a sewer problem?

They can point to a shared drainage issue, but an assessment is needed before identifying the cause.

Is a camera inspection included?

Camera equipment and inspection scope have not been confirmed for this website. Ask what investigation methods are available for your line.

Will every sewer problem require digging?

No particular repair method is assumed. The cause, location, access, and pipe condition guide the discussion.

Does clearing a blockage repair damaged pipe?

Clearing restores a path through an obstruction; it does not by itself repair a damaged section. Ask whether any further work is recommended after assessment.
