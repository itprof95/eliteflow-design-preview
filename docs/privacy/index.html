What the form asks for

A standard request collects the residential or commercial property type, your name, phone, email, service address, ZIP code, service needed, problem description, preferred date and time, and an optional photo. Commercial requests also ask for a business or organization name and optional site details, such as access arrangements and operating-hour constraints. Do not include security codes or private documents.

How the information is used

The intended purpose is to respond to your service inquiry and discuss an appointment. Checking the contact box does not subscribe you to marketing.

Current preview behavior

The approved receiving inbox is Liteflowservices@gmail.com. Automatic forwarding is paused until that inbox is verified and a delivery test succeeds. Submitting this preview form does not send or save your request on a server. Values remain in the current page, and selecting “Open email draft” passes the text to your own email application. You must send that email yourself. A selected photo is not attached automatically.

Before the form is enabled

FormSubmit is prepared to forward requests and the optional photo to the approved inbox after activation. Its documentation states that text submissions are retained for 30 days; uploaded files are not retained in its submissions archive. FormSubmit documentation and privacy terms explain the provider’s practices. No customer information is sent to FormSubmit while automatic forwarding is paused. [Business email retention, deletion process, and final privacy contact to be confirmed.]

Maps and external links

Google Maps loads automatically on the service-area page and receives connection information when it loads. The written address, DMV service information, and a separate map link remain available if the embedded map cannot display. External links and your email application have their own privacy practices. Photography and the logo are hosted with this website. No advertising or analytics scripts are included by this website.
